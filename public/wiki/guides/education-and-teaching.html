Learning and expertise

Education & Teaching

RockMundo gives characters several ways to improve: individual practice, structured lessons, tutorials, specialist mentors, formal education and learning around other musicians. As expertise develops, teaching can become a parallel career rather than progression being a one-way expense.

Beginner–IntermediateLearningTeaching

In brief

Use practice for regular self-directed development, structured education when you want focused progression, and specialist mentors or other players when their expertise matches the skill you need. Teaching becomes more useful after your character has genuine knowledge worth passing on.

Ways to learn

Practice

Flexible and closely connected to the music you perform. It uses time but can fit naturally into a musician's routine.

Lessons and tutorials

Structured learning that can give a clearer development route when you know what skill you want to improve.

Specialist tutors or mentors

Higher-expertise teachers can be valuable for focused progression, but availability, requirements and price can differ.

Formal education

Universities and other organised study routes exchange larger schedule commitments for deliberate long-term development.

Choose the method, not just the reward

The best learning route is the one that fits your wider career. A cheap lesson is not cheap if it creates a travel clash; a fast-looking option is not useful if it develops a skill you do not use.

  1. Pick the skill. Start from the actual skill tree and the activity you want to improve.
  2. Check prerequisites. Make sure an advanced path is available or identify the foundation you still need.
  3. Compare time and cost. Include travel and other commitments, not just the listed lesson price.
  4. Book around important events. Avoid sacrificing gig, rehearsal or recording readiness for an unnecessary lesson slot.
  5. Use what you learn. Reinforce development through practice and real activities.

Mentors and specialist discovery

Some teaching opportunities are more specialised than a general lesson. Mentors can focus on particular skills or career areas, and some may have requirements, locations or availability conditions.

Do not chase a famous mentor simply because they exist.A specialist is most useful when their focus matches the path your character is actively developing.

Teaching other players

Teaching turns progression into a social and economic system. A capable character can help another player's development while building their own teaching history and earning income where the system supports it.

  • Teach skills in which your character has meaningful expertise.
  • Keep teaching commitments compatible with your own gigs and band schedule.
  • Use teaching as a side career if it supports your identity rather than allowing it to crowd out the music you care about.
  • Remember that the student still needs time and follow-up practice; one lesson does not replace development.

Learning inside a band

Bandmates create another useful context for development. Sharing knowledge can help a group become more balanced, but the most efficient band is not necessarily one where every member learns every instrument. Complementary specialists often create a stronger ensemble.

Progression without formula chasing

Mechanics without spoilersLesson XP values, mentor bonuses and efficiency curves can be rebalanced. The Compendium therefore focuses on prerequisites, time, cost and relevance instead of publishing an optimal training calendar.
